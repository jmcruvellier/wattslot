# WattSlot

Plans the use of household appliances and the home battery around solar production and electricity prices. Local, no cloud service required.

- Learns your appliances from Home Assistant's Energy dashboard.
- Plans tomorrow and the week ahead with the solar forecast and your electricity contract.
- Answers Assist's questions about the plan.
- Hot backups and automatic restart, like any Home Assistant add-on.

See the **Documentation** tab for requirements and settings.
